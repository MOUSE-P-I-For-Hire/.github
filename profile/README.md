## About MOUSE: P.I. For Hire

MOUSE: P.I. For Hire is a first-person action game that blends classic noir detective storytelling with stylized cartoon visuals inspired by early animation. Players step into the role of a private investigator navigating a city filled with crime, mystery, and dangerous encounters.

The game combines exploration, shooting mechanics, and investigation elements. Players follow leads, interact with environments, uncover clues, and engage in fast-paced combat against enemies using a variety of weapons and tools.

With its unique visual style, narrative-driven structure, and action-focused gameplay, MOUSE: P.I. For Hire delivers an experience built around mystery, combat, and cinematic presentation.
